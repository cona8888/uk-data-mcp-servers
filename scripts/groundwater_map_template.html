How to read this map

mAOD means metres above Ordnance Datum. It does not give groundwater depth below a proposed site. The source responses do not supply aquifer or screen-depth information, or a surveyed ground level for your site.

Each station has one latest available observation from its advertised series. Dipped and logged observations, observation dates and per-reading quality remain separate. A series labelled “Qualified” can contain a reading marked “Unchecked”. Near-colocated shallow/deep or dipped/logged station records are retained separately; numbered callouts do not move their mapped coordinates.
